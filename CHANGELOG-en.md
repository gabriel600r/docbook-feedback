# What's new in DoctorBook

## 0.12.0 — October 2026
- Discounts: change the price charged when booking or from the details, with −10%, −20% or no charge.
- Birthdays: add your patients' birthdays and the agenda reminds you on the day, with a greeting ready to send on WhatsApp.
- More room for free: up to 50 patients, 15 services and 3 doctors, now with ads. PRO removes them and has no limits.

## 0.11.2 — September 2026
- Voice booking understands “half past” and “quarter to”, nicknames like Bill or Peggy, and asks which one when two people share a name.
- The alarm button and the notification names now follow the app’s language.
- WhatsApp: the built-in messages are now written for patients. Any you edited stay as you left them.

## 0.11.1 — September 2026
- Voice booking now understands English, Portuguese and Italian: dictate the appointment in the app language.

## 0.11.0 — September 2026
- Your appointments are no longer capped on the free plan: book your whole week.
- New metrics: what you earned, what pays best and who comes back, at a glance. It now counts only work already done, so the numbers may come out lower than before.
- The sample services no longer use up your free slots and come in your language.
- The app now starts in your phone language (Spanish, English, Portuguese or Italian).
- The welcome tour is shorter and no longer comes back on every launch.
- The nightly summary keeps coming even if you don't open the app, and stays quiet when there's nothing.

## 0.10.4 — August 2026
- We are now ready for Android 16, the latest system release, so the app keeps working just as well on newer phones

## 0.10.3 — July 2026
- Fixed: in some cases the PRO subscription failed to load and the subscribe button stayed disabled

## 0.10.2 — July 2026
- Google Play purchase system updated to the latest version: PRO subscriptions safer and up to date

## 0.10.1 — May 2026
- Fixed an unexpected crash when using voice input

## 0.10.0 — April 2026
- App now available in Italian

## 0.9.0 — April 2026
- App now available in Portuguese (Brazil/Portugal)

## 0.6.0 — April 2026
- App now available in English and Spanish
- New options menu on appointment details
- Currency picker with search
- Bulk complete appointments from agenda
- Search for patients when creating an appointment
- Contact import without duplicates
