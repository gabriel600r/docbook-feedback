# Privacy Policy - DocBook

**Last updated:** April 9, 2026

**Developer:** Gabriel Egea — EgeaINC

## Overview

DocBook is an appointment booking app for healthcare professionals. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection and Storage

**DocBook does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.** All data is stored locally on your device only.

### Data stored locally on your device:
- Patient names, phone numbers, and email addresses
- Appointment details (date, time, service, price)
- Services/procedures and their prices
- App settings and preferences

### Data NOT collected:
- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use tracking technologies
- We do not collect location data
- We do not access your camera
- We do not collect advertising identifiers
- We do not store medical records, diagnoses, or health information

## Permissions

DocBook requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Contacts** | Optional. To import contacts from your phone's address book into the app |
| **Microphone** | Optional. For voice appointment input (speech-to-text) |
| **Notifications** | Optional. To send appointment reminders |
| **Biometric** | Optional. To secure app access with fingerprint or face recognition |

All permissions are optional and requested only when you use the related feature. The app functions without granting any of these permissions.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Billing
If you subscribe to DocBook PRO, the payment is processed by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### WhatsApp
DocBook can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to remind patients of their appointments. This is done through Android's standard intent system — DocBook does not access your WhatsApp data or send messages automatically.

## Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with any third parties. Your data stays on your device.

## Important Medical Disclaimer

DocBook is an appointment scheduling tool only. It is NOT a medical records system (EMR/EHR) and should not be used to store sensitive health information, diagnoses, or treatment plans. The app does not comply with HIPAA or similar healthcare data regulations as it is designed solely for appointment management.

## Backup and Restore

DocBook offers a backup feature that exports your data to a local file on your device. You control where this file is stored and shared. We do not have access to your backups.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:
- Deleting individual records within the app
- Clearing the app's data from Android Settings
- Uninstalling the app

## Children's Privacy

DocBook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

# Política de Privacidad - DocBook

**Última actualización:** 9 de abril de 2026

**Desarrollador:** Gabriel Egea — EgeaINC

## Resumen

DocBook es una aplicación de agenda de turnos para profesionales de la salud. Tu privacidad es importante para nosotros. Esta política explica cómo manejamos tu información.

## Recopilación y almacenamiento de datos

**DocBook NO recopila, transmite ni almacena datos personales en servidores externos.** Todos los datos se almacenan localmente en tu dispositivo.

### Datos almacenados localmente en tu dispositivo:
- Nombres, teléfonos y emails de pacientes
- Detalles de turnos (fecha, hora, servicio, precio)
- Servicios/prácticas y sus precios
- Configuraciones de la app

### Datos que NO recopilamos:
- No recopilamos datos de uso ni analíticas
- No usamos tecnologías de rastreo
- No recopilamos datos de ubicación
- No accedemos a tu cámara
- No recopilamos identificadores publicitarios
- No almacenamos historias clínicas, diagnósticos ni información de salud

## Aviso médico importante

DocBook es una herramienta de gestión de turnos únicamente. NO es un sistema de historias clínicas (HCE) y no debe usarse para almacenar información sensible de salud, diagnósticos o planes de tratamiento.

## Permisos

| Permiso | Propósito |
|---|---|
| **Contactos** | Opcional. Para importar contactos del teléfono |
| **Micrófono** | Opcional. Para carga de turnos por voz |
| **Notificaciones** | Opcional. Para recordatorios de turnos |
| **Biometría** | Opcional. Para bloqueo con huella o reconocimiento facial |

Todos los permisos son opcionales. La app funciona sin otorgar ninguno.

## Servicios de terceros

### Google Play Billing
Si te suscribís a DocBook PRO, el pago es procesado por Google Play. No tenemos acceso a tu información de pago.

### WhatsApp
DocBook puede abrir WhatsApp con un mensaje pre-armado. No accedemos a tus datos de WhatsApp ni enviamos mensajes automáticamente.

## Compartir datos

NO compartimos tus datos con terceros. Tus datos permanecen en tu dispositivo.

## Eliminación de datos

Podés eliminar todos tus datos en cualquier momento desde la app, limpiando los datos desde Ajustes de Android, o desinstalando la app.
